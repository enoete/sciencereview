Forces & Motion
Push it. Pull it. Make it MOVE. 🚀
Imagine This ✨

It's lunchtime at CFBC Primary. Zalia pushes open the cafeteria door — that's a PUSH. Markisio pulls his chair out — that's a PULL. Adiaha spins her water bottle on the table — that's a TWIST (Torsion!). Every single move your body makes involves a FORCE! 🍽️

🤜 What is a Force?

A force is a push or a pull that can:

  • ▶️ Start an object moving
  • ⏹️ Stop a moving object
  • ↩️ Change the direction of motion
  • 🔄 Change the shape of an object
Quick Analogy

Forces are like the DJ at a party — they control what moves, when it moves, and how fast! 🎧

🏃 Push vs. Pull
🙌
PUSH
Moves object away from you
Examples: Pushing a shopping cart, kicking a ball, pressing a button
🤲
PULL
Brings object toward you
Examples: Pulling a drawer, opening a door, reeling in a fishing line

👋 Contact Forces

Must touch the object to affect it

Friction 🛹 Air Resistance 💨 Applied Force 💪 Normal Force ⬆️
Real Talk

Shiloh slides in socks on the hallway floor — friction slows her down when the socks grip the floor. That's a contact force! ✨

🌌 Non-Contact Forces

Work WITHOUT touching the object

Gravity 🌍 Magnetism 🧲 Electric Force ⚡
Real Talk

Vihan drops his pencil — it falls to the floor WITHOUT the Earth touching it. That's gravity! A non-contact force that pulls everything with mass toward Earth's center. 🌐

🌍 Gravity — The Universe's Magnet
  • Pulls objects toward the center of the Earth
  • It's why things fall down — always
  • Acts on everything that has mass
  • It's a non-contact force
  • The bigger the mass, the stronger the gravity
Analogy Alert!

Think of gravity like an invisible hand that's always gently pulling everything downward. It's why Amar'J's juice box falls off the lunch table, Jordan's ball comes back down after being thrown, and why YOU don't float off into space during recess! 🚀

Forces on Structures
What goes on INSIDE buildings, bridges & bones 🦴
Did You Know?

When Luke sits on his chair, Sydney leans on a wall, or Andre stretches a rubber band — forces are happening inside the material itself! These are called internal forces.

The 4 Internal Forces 🔍
Force What It Does Body Analogy Real Example
COMPRESSION 🧱 Squashing / pushing inward — the material pushes BACK Like hugging a pillow — it squashes but pushes back Coryn sits on a chair → legs push back from inside so they don't crush
TENSION 🪢 Stretching / pulling apart — material pulls BACK together Like stretching a rubber band — it wants to go back A bridge cable is pulled tight — the inside of the cable resists snapping
SHEAR ✂️ One part tries to slide past another — material resists sliding Like slipping on ice — your shoe resists sliding sideways Strong wind hits a wall — layers inside the wall grab each other to not slide apart
TORSION 🌀 Twisting — material resists the twist Like wringing out a wet towel — it fights the twist Adiaha twists a bottle cap → inside the cap fights to hold its shape
🧱 Compression

Chair legs, bridge supports, building columns all deal with compression from the weight above.

🪢 Tension

Bridge cables, guitar strings, and tug-of-war ropes all experience tension — being pulled from both ends!

✂️ Shear

Scissors use shear force to cut paper. Earthquakes cause shear in the ground and buildings!

🌀 Torsion

Torsion hits bridges in heavy wind, your doorknob when you turn it, and even Zamari's juice cap! 🌀

Inside vs. Outside 📦
🔴 External Forces (from outside)
  • Gravity pulling a bridge down
  • Wind pushing on a building
  • People walking on a floor
  • A car driving across a bridge
🔵 Internal Forces (from inside)
  • Tension in a bridge cable
  • Compression in a column
  • Shear in a wall panel
  • Torsion in a twisted bolt

🪖 Protective Equipment — Science Saves Lives!

Engineers use their knowledge of forces to design gear that absorbs and spreads force so our bodies aren't damaged.

⛑️
Helmet
Spreads the impact force across the whole helmet so your skull doesn't take it all
🦵
Knee Pads
Absorb compression force when you fall so your knee cap doesn't crush
🚗
Seatbelt
Applies tension across your chest to stop your body flying forward during a crash
Quick Connect

When Aeva puts on a helmet to ride her bike, the foam inside uses compression to absorb the impact, and the straps use tension to keep it on. Physics is literally protecting her brain! 🧠✨

Natural Structures
Nature was an engineer FIRST 🦴🐚
Mind-Blowing Fact

A spider web can hold thousands of times its own weight! A spider web is actually one of the strongest structures in nature. Engineers study spider silk to design better cables and body armor! 🕸️ Nature's been doing engineering for millions of years before humans caught up.

🐚
Shell

Curved shape distributes compression forces — that's why it's so hard to crack!

🦴
Bones

Hollow inside = light weight. Outer layer handles compression. Inner structure resists shear.

🌳
Tree Trunk

Flexible enough to survive torsion from wind. Roots handle tension — holding the tree down!

🕸️
Spider Web

Incredible tension in the silk threads. Can stretch 40% before snapping!

🐝
Honeycomb

Hexagonal shape is the strongest pattern per gram — engineers use it in airplane wings!

🌊
Cave

Arch shape converts downward force into outward compression — same trick used in stone bridges!

Simple Machines
Working smarter, not harder! 💪🧠
The Big Idea

Simple machines help you do the same amount of work with less effort — either by changing the amount of force needed OR the direction of the force. Think of them as nature's cheat codes for doing hard jobs! 🕹️

⚖️
Lever
A rigid bar that rotates around a fixed point called a fulcrum. Move the fulcrum closer to the heavy object → easier to lift!
🎯 Seesaw • Crowbar • Bottle opener • Scissors
Kosi and Viktoria on the seesaw — the fulcrum (middle point) makes it easy to lift each other!
🔘
Pulley
A wheel with a rope or chain. Lets you lift heavy loads by pulling down instead of up — much easier on your muscles!
🎯 Flagpole • Crane • Well • Window blinds
When the school flag goes up, a pulley is doing the work — Miquea pulls the rope DOWN, the flag goes UP!
📐
Inclined Plane
A flat surface set at an angle (a ramp). Spreads the force needed over a longer distance — so you use less force at a time!
🎯 Ramp • Slide • Loading dock • Ski slope
A wheelchair ramp is an inclined plane — Anna can push her grandmother up a ramp much more easily than lifting her straight up!
🚗
Wheel & Axle
A large wheel attached to a smaller cylinder (axle). Turning the big wheel needs less force and the axle turns a lot faster!
🎯 Car wheels • Doorknob • Steering wheel • Fan
Leila turns the big round doorknob (wheel) and the small axle inside the door clicks open with minimal effort!
🪓
Wedge
Two inclined planes joined together to form a sharp edge. Converts forward force into a splitting or cutting force sideways!
🎯 Axe • Knife • Chisel • Zipper • Nail
When Adiaha cuts her birthday cake, the knife blade (a wedge) pushes the cake apart sideways as it moves downward!
🔩
Screw
An inclined plane wrapped around a cylinder! The spiral shape converts rotational force into forward motion or a super tight grip.
🎯 Jar lid • Bolt • Drill bit • Bottle cap
Zamari turns a jar lid round and round — the screw thread converts that rotating motion into the lid gripping super tight!

Energy
The power behind EVERYTHING ✨🌍
The Big Definition

Energy is the ability to do work or cause change. Without energy, nothing happens — no movement, no heat, no sound, no life! Adiaha uses chemical energy (from food 🍕) to perform on stage. Her voice is sound energy. The spotlight above her is light energy. Energy is EVERYWHERE!

Mechanical Energy

Shiloh running at Sports Day → her body is using mechanical energy. A spinning fan, a rolling ball, gears turning in a clock — all mechanical!

When Miquea's mom cooks rice on the stove, the fire heats the particles in the pot faster and faster. That's thermal (heat) energy!

🔊

When Adiaha sings her calypso, her vocal cords vibrate → the air vibrates → the vibrations travel all the way to our ears! That's sound energy!

The power that makes Vihan's phone light up, Jordan's fan spin, and the school's lights glow. Electrons moving through wires!

🍕
Chemical Energy

Stored in food, petrol, batteries, and fireworks! When Zamari eats lunch, chemical energy is released from the food to power his body!

Potential & Kinetic Energy
Stored vs. Moving — the ultimate energy duo! 🎢
🏋️
Potential Energy (PE)

Stored energy — energy that is waiting to be released. Like potential means "possible" — it has the potential to do work!

Stretched rubber band Water at top of waterfall Roller coaster at the top A boulder on a hill
🏃
Kinetic Energy (KE)

Energy of motion — any object that's moving has kinetic energy. The faster it moves, the more kinetic energy it has!

Rolling ball Flying bird Roller coaster going down Splashing water

The Roller Coaster Story 🎢

Imagine Adiaha, Kosi, Leila and Sydney are on a roller coaster:

  • 🔵 At the very top → maximum POTENTIAL energy (highest height, barely moving)
  • 🟠 Racing down → PE converts to KINETIC energy (gaining speed!)
  • 🔵 Going up the next hill → KE converts back to PE (slowing down)
  • ✨ The energy keeps switching back and forth — it's never created or destroyed, just transformed!

Heat Transfer
3 ways heat gets around 🌡️
🍴
Conduction — Touch to Touch
Heat moves through direct contact
▼

Conduction happens when heat travels through a material or between two objects that are touching. The fast-moving particles bump into slower ones and pass the energy along.

Analogy

Coryn puts a metal spoon in her hot chocolate ☕ — after a few seconds, the handle is warm! Heat moved from the hot soup UP through the spoon to Coryn's fingers. That's conduction!

Good conductors: metals (copper, iron, steel) | Poor conductors (insulators): wood, plastic, rubber

🌊
Convection — Currents of Heat
Heat moves through liquids and gases
▼

Convection happens in fluids (liquids and gases). Hot particles become less dense and rise up, while cooler particles sink down — creating a circular current.

Analogy

When Andre's mom boils soup 🍲 — the hot soup at the bottom rises up, the cooler soup at the top sinks down. This keeps going in a big circle (convection current) until the whole pot is hot!

Same thing happens with air — warm air near a heater RISES, cool air FALLS. That's why the top floor of a building is always warmer! 🏠

☀️
Radiation — Heat Through Space
Heat travels as waves — no material needed!
▼

Radiation is heat transferred as electromagnetic waves. It's the ONLY type that can travel through the vacuum of space — no material needed!

Analogy

The Sun is 150 million km away — there's nothing between us and the Sun but empty space. Yet Adiaha feels warm at Sports Day! That's radiation — the Sun's heat energy travels as waves through empty space and warms us up. You also feel radiation from a campfire even without touching it! 🔥

Renewable Energy
The planet's clean energy solutions 🌍💚
Why Does It Matter?

Our planet is getting warmer because humans burn too many fossil fuels that release greenhouse gases. Renewable energy is our superpower — it can be replaced naturally in a short time and creates WAY less pollution. The future Adiaha, Kosi, and all your classmates will live in depends on switching to renewables NOW! 🌱

Conserve & Preserve
Protecting our planet for future Adiahas! 🌿
💡
CONSERVE = Use Wisely

Using resources carefully so they last longer. Not wasting what we have.

  • 🔆 Turn off lights when leaving a room
  • 💧 Turn off the tap while brushing teeth
  • ♻️ Reuse containers instead of buying new
  • 🚶 Walk instead of drive for short trips
  • 📱 Unplug chargers when not in use
Adiaha reminds her family to turn off lights at home. That's conservation! Small acts × millions of people = HUGE impact 🌍
🛡️
PRESERVE = Protect from Harm

Keeping natural resources and environments safe from destruction.

  • 🌳 National Parks and protected forests
  • 🐠 Marine reserves for coral reefs
  • 🏔️ No-build zones in sensitive areas
  • 🌿 Laws against cutting down rainforests
  • 🐢 Wildlife protection programs
St. Kitts's coral reefs are PRESERVED by marine protected areas. Without them, Leila and future generations won't see them! 🐠
♻️ The 3 Rs — In Order of Importance!
🚫
REDUCE
Use less in the first place. Best option! Don't buy things you don't need.
E.g.: Buy one water bottle and refill it, not 100 plastic ones
🔄
REUSE
Use items again instead of throwing away. Second best!
E.g.: Use both sides of paper, donate old clothes, reuse containers
♻️
RECYCLE
Turn waste into new materials. Better than landfill but uses energy.
E.g.: Aluminum cans, glass bottles, cardboard, some plastics
Plant Trees = Save the Planet

Trees are nature's air filters! They absorb CO₂ (the main greenhouse gas) and release oxygen. One tree can absorb about 22 kg of CO₂ per year. If every student in Adiaha's class planted just one tree per year, that's a lot of climate action from one classroom! 🌳🌍

Also: save water 💧 and electricity ⚡ every single day — these all connect back to reducing fossil fuel use and protecting our environment.
